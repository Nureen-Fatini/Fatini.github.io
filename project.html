My Reflection

This project has been a truly valuable learning experience, helping me think more critically, work better with others, and come up with different ways to solve problems. Using design thinking, I was able to approach challenges in a structured yet creative way, making sure my ideas were both practical and meaningful. Collaborating with my teammates showed me how important teamwork, adaptability, and time management are, as we had to balance creativity with what was actually possible within our deadline. Beyond that, this experience reminded me of how technology can create positive change by making opportunities more accessible to everyone. It also made me realize the need to keep improving my communication and networking skills, as they are key to building strong connections for future projects. Overall, this project has pushed me to grow, both as a team player and as someone who wants to make an impact through technology.
